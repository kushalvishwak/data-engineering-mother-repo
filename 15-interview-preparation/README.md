# 15 · Interview preparation

Use each prompt to practice concise explanation followed by evidence and trade-offs. Answers are starting points: state assumptions, distinguish guarantees from configuration, and connect decisions to workload measurements.

Topics: Python, SQL, PySpark, Spark, Databricks, Azure, AWS, Snowflake, system design, data modeling, and scenario-based discussion.

## Answer format

For each high-value prompt, practice a short direct answer, then explain assumptions and semantics, give a concrete example, name a common failure mode, and close with a senior-level follow-up. Avoid reciting product names without tying them to workload evidence or a guarantee.

Use [`20-interview-projects/README.md`](../20-interview-projects/README.md) to practice complete designs under time constraints. For architecture prompts, state requirements and SLOs before drawing components; for operational prompts, distinguish detection, containment, recovery, reconciliation, and prevention.
